Before you start

careAI gives general self-care information for minor, non-emergency issues only. It does not diagnose conditions and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

If you upload a photo or describe symptoms, that information is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to be processed. This app does not store your messages or photos on its own servers — each conversation is stateless, and nothing is saved after you close the page.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, please contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital instead of using this tool.
